# TID-481: Lore Hooks in the Orphan Docs + story.md Proposal

Goal: [GID-127](goal.md) · Type: agent (+ one human-action proposal) · Status: done

## Problem

The cosmology is useless if a reader working on the Blight has to know to go
looking for it in `magic-system.md`. Each affected system needs the account at
the point of use — without restating the frame, which would immediately drift.

## Changes Made

A `## Lore` section added to each of the four orphan docs, sitting between Key
Features and How It Works, each opening with a pointer to the full frame:

- **`docs/agent/ley-lines.md`** — veins are paths essence wore for itself; the
  cyan gradient is flow density; speed boost and Attuned mana share one cause;
  Mana Wells are surplus surfacing at crossings, which is why they spawn only at
  intersections and why they never refill.
- **`docs/agent/blight-system.md`** — an opened vein, bleeding outward and never
  contracting; over-saturation rather than curse, hence +5 HP rather than some
  new hostility; the Redemption payout justified as the most life-aligned act
  available; the pure-function determinism reframed as in-fiction correct (the
  bleed is mechanical, not malicious).
- **`docs/agent/ancient-colossi.md`** — apertures, not grips; all five worked
  before they stopped; five unrelated attempts explains five unrelated variants;
  location-derived naming explained as regions guessing at a forgotten name.
- **`docs/agent/card-cantrips.md`** — a card is a creature's essence-pattern;
  four is where the pattern stops being noise and starts imposing; the effects
  are the creature's own trick, which is why the deck shapes the world.

Each hook explains its own system's constants and defers everything else. No hook
restates the frame.

## Human-Action Proposal — story canon

`docs/human/story.md` is human-owned and was **not edited**. The following
connect the cosmology to the plot and need explicit approval before anyone writes
them in. Precedent: the GID-108 story pack was drafted by the agent and approved
in-file on 2026-07-02.

### 1. Maiteln is a hearth-worker *(recommended)*

Makes canon what the cosmology already assumes. He conjures horses, smooths
roads, tidies rooms and cooks, and never duels — because hearth-work does a
hundred useful things badly and no destructive thing at all. Resolves the
contradiction between the story's only wizard and the four-type system without a
fifth type. Costs nothing; changes no existing dialogue.

### 2. The Blight is the sign the prophecy names *(recommended)*

The prophecy currently gives Maiteln urgency the player cannot see. The Blight is
already spreading visibly in the open world, on a daily timer, with no plot
connection at all. Tying them makes the ambient system into evidence: the reason
Maiteln is in a hurry is the thing the player keeps walking past.

### 3. The Martarquas learned to open veins *(offered)*

Answers a question the prophecy raises and does not resolve — how a crushed tribe
comes back *worse*. The alliance crushed them the first time; it did not, and
could not, close what they had opened. This also explains why "never fully
destroyed" is the ominous phrasing rather than a technicality.

The stronger version, if wanted: the alliance's victory is what made the hearts
permanent, so the last war's win condition is the current war's cause. That is a
notably darker reading of the founding myth and is offered, not recommended —
it is a real change to the tone of the backstory and should be a human call.

### 4. Isfig *(no proposal)*

Nothing in the cosmology needs him to be a mage, and inventing an affinity for a
character described only as "about 6 foot, stocky, not overly smart" would add
nothing. Left alone deliberately.
