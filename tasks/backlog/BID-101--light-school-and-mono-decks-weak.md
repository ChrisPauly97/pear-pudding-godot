# BID-101: Light school trails; mono-school decks not viable

**Category:** design-inconsistency
**Discovered During:** GID-183 / TID-771

## Description

After GID-183 (verdant + rift Allies) and TID-771 tuning, the school-matched light deck still trails the default deck by 20–35 pp in most biomes (its technique/spell fill is draw-heavy), and pure mono-school decks are still not viable, so balance band (a) (school-matched within ±25 pp) stays report-only. Desert dark/verdant hit 100 % largely from cactus worm's profile.

## Evidence

TID-771 table (win %, default/light/dark/verdant/rift): forest 50/14/50/43/21, scorched 43/14/64/64/29, mountains 50/21/64/57/14. See `tasks/goals/GID-183--magic-school-allies/TID-771--mono-school-rebalance.md`, `docs/agent/balance-sim.md`.

## Suggested Resolution

Give light more damage/board presence (light Allies or damage spells), revisit rift's low numbers, then make band (a) gating. Consider raising fights per cell to cut noise.
